ACME Corporation — Employee Handbook
=====================================
Version 3.2 | Effective Date: January 1, 2024

SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION
------------------------
Welcome to ACME Corporation. This handbook outlines the policies, benefits,
and expectations for all full-time employees. Please read this document
carefully and retain it for future reference.

SECTION 2: LEAVE POLICY
------------------------

2.1 Annual Leave
Annual leave is a paid benefit provided to all permanent employees.

- All full-time employees are entitled to 24 annual leave days per calendar year.
- Annual leave is accrued at the rate of 2 days per month.
- Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the next calendar year,
  subject to a maximum carry-forward of 10 days.
- Annual leave must be requested at least 3 days in advance using the HR Portal.
  Requests submitted less than 3 days before the intended leave date may be denied
  unless exceptional circumstances apply.
- Leave requests are subject to manager approval and business requirements.
- Employees who resign or are terminated will receive a payout for any unused
  accrued annual leave at their final salary rate.

2.2 Sick Leave
ACME Corporation provides paid sick leave to support employees during illness.

- All full-time employees are entitled to 12 sick leave days per calendar year.
- Sick leave does not carry forward to the next year; unused sick leave is forfeited.
- For absences of 3 or more consecutive days, a medical certificate from a
  registered medical practitioner is required.
- Sick leave should be reported to your manager before 9:00 AM on the day of absence.
- Sick leave may not be taken immediately before or after annual leave without a
  valid medical certificate.

2.3 Public Holidays
Employees are entitled to all gazetted public holidays in their country of employment.
If an employee is required to work on a public holiday, they will receive 1.5x their
daily rate as compensation or a substitute day off, at the company's discretion.

2.4 Unpaid Leave
Employees may apply for unpaid leave after exhausting all accrued annual leave.
Unpaid leave is subject to manager and HR Director approval. A maximum of 30
consecutive calendar days of unpaid leave is permitted without affecting employment
continuity.

SECTION 3: CODE OF CONDUCT
---------------------------

3.1 Workplace Behaviour
All employees are expected to conduct themselves professionally and respectfully
at all times. Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind will not be
tolerated and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

3.2 Dress Code
Business casual attire is required in the office. Client-facing roles may require
formal business dress on specific occasions. Casual Fridays are permitted as long
as clothing remains appropriate for a professional environment.

3.3 Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose proprietary company information, trade secrets, or
client data to any unauthorised party during or after their employment. Violation
of this policy may result in legal action.

SECTION 4: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
-----------------------------------

4.1 Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted annually in December. Employees and managers
set Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) at the start of each quarter. Underperformance
triggers a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) with a 90-day review period.

4.2 Promotions
Promotions are merit-based and subject to available budget. Employees must have
completed at least 12 months in their current role to be eligible for promotion
consideration.

SECTION 5: BENEFITS
--------------------

5.1 Health Insurance
The company provides group health insurance through TrustLife Insurance.
Details of coverage, limits, and claim procedures are available in the
Insurance Policy Document provided separately.

5.2 Provident Fund
The company contributes 12% of the employee's basic salary to the Provident Fund
(PF) in accordance with applicable regulations.

5.3 Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)
ACME offers a confidential Employee Assistance Programme that provides counselling,
legal advice, and financial guidance. Contact HR for access details.

SECTION 6: GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE
--------------------------------
Employees who wish to raise a grievance should first attempt to resolve the matter
with their immediate manager. If unresolved, a formal grievance may be submitted
in writing to HR within 5 working days of the incident.

SECTION 7: TERMINATION POLICY
-------------------------------
Either party may terminate employment by providing notice as follows:
- During probation (first 3 months): 1 week's notice.
- After probation up to 2 years' service: 1 month's notice.
- After 2 years' service: 3 months' notice.

Payment in lieu of notice may be made at the company's discretion.
